## Favorite Series: Game of Thrones

*Game of Thrones* is an HBO fantasy series based on the books **A Song of Ice and Fire** by George R. R. Martin. It ran for 8 seasons from 2011 to 2019.

### Why I like it

- Complex characters
  - No one is fully good or fully bad
  - Anyone can die at any time
- Political drama and power struggles
- Great world-building

> "Winter is coming."
> Words of House Stark
